My offerings

Paths toward presence and compassion

Each process is a space of care, clarity and trust. We move at your pace, with a pragmatic and grounded presence, so that what you discover can be integrated into your everyday life.

01

Compassionate Inquiry

A therapeutic conversation that goes beyond the story we tell ourselves. With curiosity and compassion, we explore what lies beneath a difficulty: the beliefs, emotions and sensations in the body that were once a way of protecting ourselves.

By noticing these patterns with kindness, consultants find new clarity, reconnect with their inner child, and open room for different choices.

  • Somatic awareness: listening to what the body holds
  • Trauma-sensitive, judgment-free space
Format
Individual sessions
Where
Puerto Varas or online
Language
Spanish or English

02

Family Constellations

We carry our family within us: its stories, its silences, its loyalties. Family Constellations offer a systemic view of these bonds, revealing hidden dynamics that may be repeating in our relationships, health or work.

When each member of the system finds their place, a new order becomes possible, and with it, relief and strength to move forward.

  • Understanding inherited patterns
  • Reconciliation with one's history
  • Individual work with figures, or in a group
Format
Individual or group
Where
Puerto Varas or online
Language
Spanish or English

03

Cacao Ceremonies, Meditations & Mindfulness

Ceremonial cacao is a facilitator for opening the heart. In these ceremonies and guided meditations we create a safe container to slow down, feel what is here, and explore emotions with gentleness.

Carolina trained for over 1,200 hours as an apprentice of Keith, a cacao shaman, in Guatemala, and brings that experience to each circle.

  • Cacao ceremonies for groups and individuals
  • Structured guided meditations
  • Mindfulness practices to bring into daily life
Format
Group circles or individual
Where
Puerto Varas and on request
Language
Spanish or English

04

Hypnosis & Regression

A guided journey into your inner world, the place where memories, habits and deep emotional responses are held. Through a state of focused relaxation we reach the unconscious, where certain patterns can be understood and behaviours gently reprogrammed.

Exploring these spaces with curiosity and care, consultants come to see past experiences in a new light, make peace with their story, and live in the present with greater freedom.

  • A gentle way in to understand memories and beliefs
  • Reprogramming patterns from a place of calm
  • A state of deep relaxation in which you always stay in control
Format
Individual sessions
Where
Puerto Varas or online
Language
Spanish or English
